Syncing Outlook Monthly Invoices

To safely gather monthly invoice PDFs sent out via Outlook to customers and contractors without giving out passwords:

Method 1: One-Click Desktop Sync

Double-click sync_invoices.cmd in your project directory. It connects directly to your desktop Outlook via MAPI, finds invoice emails with PDF attachments, downloads them to uploads/inbox, and registers them automatically.

Method 2: Web Drag-and-Drop

Click "Batch Drop Invoices" in the Pay Apps & Invoices tab, and drop invoice PDFs. The system extracts the invoice numbers and balances automatically.
